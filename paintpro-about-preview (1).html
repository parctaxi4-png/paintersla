About PaintPro

A painting company built on a simple idea: good paint work is mostly preparation, patience and honest communication.

Our story

How PaintPro Started

PaintPro began as a small crew handling interior repaints for local homeowners. Most of the jobs we were called to fix had the same problem: the paint itself was fine, but the preparation underneath had been rushed or skipped entirely.

So we built our process around the unglamorous parts first: washing, scraping, sanding, patching, caulking and priming. Only when the surface is right does the finish coat go on. That approach takes a little longer at the start of a job and saves our customers from repainting again a few years later.

Today we handle interior and exterior painting for homes, cabinet refinishing, and commercial projects of different sizes, all run on the same standards described on this page.

What guides us

Our Values

Do the prep properly

A finish is only as good as the surface under it. We never paint over loose, dirty or damaged surfaces just to finish faster.

Say it straight

If we find rot, moisture or damage that changes the job, we tell you before we paint over it, with options and honest pricing.

Respect the property

We mask, cover and protect floors, furniture, fixtures and landscaping, and we leave every site clean at the end of the day.

Use the right product

Primer and paint choices are matched to the surface, the room and the weather the coating will face, not chosen by habit.

Finish what we start

Every project ends with a joint walkthrough. Touch-ups we agree on are completed before the job is closed.

Keep learning

Coatings and tools change. We test new products on real surfaces first and only use them on customer projects once proven.

How we work

Our Painting Process

The same six steps on every project, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Consultation and quote

    We look at the project with you, measure the surfaces, discuss colors and products, and send a written quote with the scope clearly listed.

  2. Scheduling and colors

    We agree on dates, confirm final colors with sample patches where needed, and order materials before the start date.

  3. Protection and preparation

    Furniture and floors are covered, surfaces are washed, scraped, sanded, patched, caulked and primed as required.

  4. Painting

    Finish coats are applied in the number agreed in your quote, with drying time respected between coats.

  5. Inspection and touch-ups

    We inspect the work in good light, mark any spots together with you and complete the touch-ups.

  6. Cleanup and walkthrough

    Masking comes off, the site is cleaned, leftover paint is labeled for you, and we do a final walkthrough together.

Our promise

Service Standards

  • Written quote before work begins, with scope, products and number of coats listed
  • Surfaces washed, scraped, sanded, patched and primed as needed before painting
  • Floors, furniture, fixtures and landscaping protected on every job
  • Work areas tidied at the end of each day, not only at the end of the project
  • Lead-safe practices followed on older properties where lead paint may be present
  • Final walkthrough with the customer and agreed touch-ups completed before closing
  • Leftover paint labeled and left with the customer for future touch-ups
  • Honest advice if a surface needs repair or replacement instead of paint
